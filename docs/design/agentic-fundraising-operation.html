1 · The operation is a flywheel

Before tools: the business. A world-class nonprofit revenue operation is one loop. Stories prove impact, impact grows an audience, audience becomes relationships, relationships become revenue, revenue creates more impact — and the loop only spins if every stage runs on cadence.

The honest diagnosis

Masi's inputs are exceptional: thousands of photos, professional designers, rigorous impact data in Postgres. The failure mode is purely rhythmic — no scheduled sends, no systematic stewardship, grants pursued ad hoc.

What separates world-class operations is rarely content or ask quality. It is cadence and stewardship — never missing a thank-you, a report, a renewal, a monthly story. That is exactly what agents do better than people, and people do better than agents at judgment and relationships. The system splits along that line.

4 · Division of labour — agents vs Jim

The doctrine: agents read everything, write only drafts; humans act on the world through one approval surface. Capability comes from the read side, safety from the write side. Autonomy is a per-agent dial that starts at zero and rises only where trust is earned (a tier-3 newsletter may eventually auto-send; a major-donor email never will).

Agents own (cadence)

  • Scanning, researching, drafting, assembling, reminding
  • Never missing a deadline, anniversary, lapse, or report
  • Mirroring funder language back in applications
  • Logging everything — the CRM writes itself

Jim owns (judgment)

  • Approving, editing, or killing every draft
  • The relationships themselves — calls, meetings, asks
  • Strategy: which funders to pursue, what the year looks like
  • Raising an agent's autonomy dial when it has earned it

6 · Additions beyond the original four (all three adopted 2026-06-12)

The original list (grant writing, discovery, CRM, marketing) covers acquisition and solicitation well. The best operations win on the rows the list misses. Stewardship Engine, Scoreboard, and Campaign Calendar are now in scope; /donate wiring is parked.

Stewardship Engine

Thank-you within 48h of any gift, scheduled impact follow-ups ("your R50k did this"), receipt issuance (incl. Section 18A certificates), lapse detection and win-back drafts.

Why: retention is the cheapest revenue in fundraising and the most cadence-sensitive — the single highest-leverage agent.

Fundraising Scoreboard

WIG-style lead measures: touches per tier per week, drafts shipped, pipeline value by stage, retention rate, cadence adherence. Lives beside the existing programme scoreboards.

Why: Masi already runs on 4DX — fundraising should be steered the same way, and agents can compute it for free.

Campaign Calendar

The year planned as campaigns and themes: year-end appeal, GivingTuesday, report seasons, programme milestones. Drives what the Marketing and Steward agents draft each week.

Why: cadence needs a clock; without a calendar, "scheduled" content has no schedule to follow.

Explicitly parked (decided 2026-06-12)

  • /donate wiring (web gifts flowing straight into the spine) — parked; revisit when we examine how donations actually arrive
  • Events and galas (donor trips, dinners) — real but not v1
  • Wealth screening / prospect research on individuals — buy later if ever
  • Volunteer and board management — different domain, same spine could host it later
  • Press/PR outreach — Marketing Agent could extend here in v2
