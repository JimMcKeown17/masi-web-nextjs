Dear Masi Friends & Family,

I hope you're all well. This is a story we know will put a smile on your face, or rather, three of them.

Right now, 19,444 children are on our programme, each one learning to read with the support of a Masi Literacy Coach. Today we want you to meet just three of them.

Meet Zeniqhame

At just 5 years old, Zeniqhame from Dorothy Tomlinson Pre School Foundation is a lively and enthusiastic learner who truly enjoys being at school. He eagerly takes part in every literacy session, showing excitement and a willingness to learn each day.

While he is still working through some challenges, Zeniqhame remains focused and consistently puts in the effort to do his best. Full of personality and wonderfully talkative, he brings a joyful energy into the classroom and uplifts those around him.

"Zeniqhame is a bright and lively 5-year-old who loves being at school and eagerly takes part in every session. While he is still facing some challenges, he remains focused and always puts in the effort to do his best. Full of personality, he is wonderfully talkative and brings a joyful energy wherever he goes, and with continued support, he is on a meaningful journey of growth and discovery."
~Nomthandazo Jordaan, Literacy Coach

Meet Lungile

Lungile Ntombela is a 5-year-old Grade R learner at Nobandla EduCare Centre. Even though she is new to the MasiLit programme, she is already performing ahead of many of her peers. She is quiet and reserved, yet very focused during sessions, and she interacts beautifully with the other children, always playing kindly.

She has a real love for story time, has a strong grasp of her three letter sounds, and is beginning to read and make sense of the stories she explores.

"Lungile is a bright and capable learner who has made a wonderful impression since joining the MasiLit programme. Although she is new, she is already working at a level that is ahead of many of her peers, as if she has been part of the programme for much longer... She has a strong grasp of her three letter sounds, she is beginning to read, and she is able to interpret stories in a meaningful way. We are currently working on building her confidence with four letter sounds, and she is responding very well."
~Liyema Plaatjie, Literacy Coach, Nobandla EduCare Centre

Meet Lisolethu

Lisolethu Ndindwa is a bright and gentle five year old in Grade R at Nobandla EduCare Centre who has recently joined the MasiLit programme. He is quiet and calm during sessions, but his willingness to learn stands out. Even when he has been absent, Lisolethu shows admirable resilience, catching up on missed work with help from his classmates.

He has a very good understanding of his vowels and is confidently developing his knowledge of three letter sounds. For a learner at this stage, he is doing exceptionally well.

"Lisolethu is a calm and gentle learner who is showing encouraging progress in his literacy journey... Even when he has been absent, Lisolethu demonstrates resilience by working hard to keep up, often supported by his classmates, which reflects both his positive attitude and willingness to grow. He has a strong understanding of his vowels and is confidently developing his knowledge of three letter sounds. Lisolethu is making meaningful strides and is showing great potential, which is something to be truly proud of."
~Liyema Plaatjie, Literacy Coach, Nobandla EduCare Centre

These are but a few of the 19,444 stories your support is enabling us to create. Every one of these children has a coach in their corner, and every one of those coaches has you to thank for the chance to do this work.
