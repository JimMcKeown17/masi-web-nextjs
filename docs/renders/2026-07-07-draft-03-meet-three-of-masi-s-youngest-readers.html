Dear Masi Friends & Family,

I hope you're all well.

Today we want you to meet three of our youngest learners. Each of them is just five years old, each is brand new to reading, and each is already showing us what's possible when a child gets a coach who shows up for them, session after session.

They are three of 19,444 children currently on programme with Masinyusane. That number only means anything because of stories like these.

Zeniqhame: joyful energy, showing up every day

At just 5 years old, Zeniqhame from Dorothy Tomlinson Pre School Foundation is a lively and enthusiastic learner who truly enjoys being at school. He eagerly takes part in every literacy session, showing excitement and a willingness to learn each day.

While he is still working through some challenges, Zeniqhame remains focused and consistently puts in the effort to do his best. Full of personality and wonderfully talkative, he brings a joyful energy into the classroom and uplifts those around him.

"Zeniqhame is a bright and lively 5-year-old who loves being at school and eagerly takes part in every session. While he is still facing some challenges, he remains focused and always puts in the effort to do his best. Full of personality, he is wonderfully talkative and brings a joyful energy wherever he goes, and with continued support, he is on a meaningful journey of growth and discovery."
~ Nomthandazo Jordaan, Literacy Coach

Lungile: already making sense of her stories

Lungile Ntombela is a 5-year-old Grade R learner at Nobandla EduCare Centre, and even though she is new to the MasiLit programme, she is already performing ahead of many of her peers. She is quiet and reserved, yet very focused during sessions, and she interacts beautifully with the other children, always playing kindly.

Lungile has a strong grasp of her three letter sounds, she is beginning to read, and she has developed a real love for story time, engaging beautifully as she makes sense of what she reads. Her coach is now building her confidence with four letter sounds, and she is responding very well.

"Lungile is a bright and capable learner who has made a wonderful impression since joining the MasiLit programme. Although she is new, she is already working at a level that is ahead of many of her peers, as if she has been part of the programme for much longer... She has a real love for story time and engages so well during these moments."
~ Liyema Plaatjie, Literacy Coach, Nobandla EduCare Centre

Lisolethu: quiet determination, strong in his vowels

Lisolethu Ndindwa is a bright and gentle five year old in Grade R at Nobandla EduCare Centre, also new to MasiLit and already showing a strong foundation for growth. He is a quiet, calm presence during sessions, but his willingness to learn stands out.

Even when he has been absent, Lisolethu shows admirable resilience, working hard to catch up with the support of his classmates. He has a very good understanding of his vowels and is confidently developing his three letter sounds, doing exceptionally well for a learner at this stage.

"Lisolethu is a calm and gentle learner who is showing encouraging progress in his literacy journey... Even when he has been absent, Lisolethu demonstrates resilience by working hard to keep up, often supported by his classmates, which reflects both his positive attitude and willingness to grow."
~ Liyema Plaatjie, Literacy Coach

Three children. Three coaches showing up for them, session after session. And 19,444 children on programme right now who deserve the exact same chance that Zeniqhame, Lungile, and Lisolethu are getting.

You make this all possible. We will never forget that. Thank you.
