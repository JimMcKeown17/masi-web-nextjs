Dear Masi Friends & Family,

I hope you're all well. This is a story we know will put a smile on your face, actually, three of them.

Right now, 19,444 children are on programme with Masinyusane, learning their letter sounds and beginning to read with the help of a Literacy Coach from their own community. Every one of them has a story. Here are three from this week.

Meet Zeniqhame

At just 5 years old, Zeniqhame from Dorothy Tomlinson Pre School Foundation is a lively and enthusiastic learner who truly enjoys being at school. He eagerly takes part in every literacy session, showing excitement and a willingness to learn each day.

While he is still working through some challenges, Zeniqhame remains focused and consistently puts in the effort to do his best. Full of personality and wonderfully talkative, he brings a joyful energy into the classroom and uplifts those around him.

"Zeniqhame is a bright and lively 5-year-old who loves being at school and eagerly takes part in every session. While he is still facing some challenges, he remains focused and always puts in the effort to do his best. Full of personality, he is wonderfully talkative and brings a joyful energy wherever he goes, and with continued support, he is on a meaningful journey of growth and discovery."
~Nomthandazo Jordaan, Literacy Coach

Meet Lungile

Lungile is a 5-year-old Grade R learner at Nobandla EduCare Centre, and even though she is new to the MasiLit programme, she is already ahead of many of her peers. She is quiet and focused during sessions, plays kindly with the other children, and has developed a real love for story time.

She has a strong grasp of her three letter sounds and has already begun her reading journey, making sense of the stories she explores.

"Lungile is a bright and capable learner who has made a wonderful impression since joining the MasiLit programme. She has a real love for story time and engages so well during these moments. She has a strong grasp of her three letter sounds, she is beginning to read, and she is able to interpret stories in a meaningful way."
~Liyema Plaatjie, Literacy Coach, Nobandla EduCare Centre

Meet Lisolethu

Lisolethu is a bright and gentle five year old, also in Grade R at Nobandla EduCare Centre, and newly joined to the MasiLit programme. He is quiet and calm during sessions, and even when he has missed a day, he works hard to catch up, often with the help of his classmates.

Lisolethu has a very good understanding of his vowels and is confidently developing his knowledge of three letter sounds, well ahead of where most learners are at this stage.

"Lisolethu is a calm and gentle learner who is showing encouraging progress in his literacy journey. Even when he has been absent, Lisolethu demonstrates resilience by working hard to keep up, often supported by his classmates, which reflects both his positive attitude and willingness to grow."
~Liyema Plaatjie, Literacy Coach, Nobandla EduCare Centre

Zeniqhame, Lungile, and Lisolethu are three of 19,444 children on programme right now. Each one has a Literacy Coach from their own community showing up for them, session after session, teaching them to read while earning an income themselves.

You make this possible. If you're able to become a monthly donor, please consider doing so. We could really use your help to keep opening doors for children like these three.

Thank you, as always, for standing with us.

With gratitude,
The Masinyusane Team
