Meet Phiwokuhle: 4 years old, and already "the aunt of the class"

Phiwokuhle is a 4-year-old learner at our Sume Centre. She already knows all her letter sounds, including ones she hasn't even been formally taught yet. Her Zazi iZandi Literacy Coach, Yonela Madlingozi, says she approaches everything with confidence and ease, and that she doesn't just sit quietly with what she knows. She shares it, helping the children around her.

"She carries within her a quiet brilliance. She knows all her letter sounds, even those she has not been formally taught, as though they come naturally to her. A true top performer, she approaches everything she does with confidence and ease. She is always willing to lend a helping hand, earning her the affectionate title of 'the aunt of the class' because of how effortlessly she supports those around her. In peer learning spaces, she does not just participate. She guides and truly excels."
~ Yonela Madlingozi, Zazi iZandi Literacy Coach

She's 4 years old. And she's already leading.

Meet Zukhanye: from Alice, Eastern Cape, to Nelson Mandela University

Zukhanye Siwa is a first year Top Learner from Alice in the Eastern Cape. His path to Nelson Mandela University, where he is now studying a BA in Humanities, went through some genuinely hard times. Masinyusane walked alongside him at every step: helping with his applications for funding and admission, and arranging accommodation and transport when he arrived in Gqeberha.

"I want to thank the Masinyusane Organization/Family for being there for me during the hard times. The possibility of me becoming a student at NMU was unlikely, but because of the presence and support of the Masinyusane family, I became one of the accepted students at Nelson Mandela University, meaning that Masinyusane made the impossible possible. Moreover, that was not the only help I received from Masi. The Masi family assisted me with my applications for both funding and admission to the institution. They also provided me with accommodation and transport when I arrived in Gqeberha. I truly appreciate the love and warmth that the Masi family has given me."
~ Zukhanye Siwa

Something that once felt impossible to him is now his everyday life. That's what support, real and consistent, can do.

Meet Sosso: from a classroom in Masi to a classroom in Korea

Sosso began with us as an Education Assistant in the Zazi iZandi pilot, showing up every day with commitment, humility, and a willingness to learn. He didn't just support the young learners in his care, he grew alongside them, building his own skills, confidence, and sense of purpose. In time, he stepped into a mentorship role, guiding others walking the same path he once walked.

Today, Sosso is teaching English in Korea. Masinyusane was a stepping stone that turned into a launchpad, one that carried him further than he might once have imagined. His story is a reminder of just how far South African youth can go when they're given the right support.

Phiwokuhle, Zukhanye, and Sosso are three of 19,444 children and young people on programme with us right now. Three names among thousands, each with their own story still being written.

You make all of this possible. We will never forget that. Thank you.

With gratitude,
The Masinyusane Team
