Purpose: records decisions taken while designing the WIG scoreboard, and open questions for Jim to confirm with the programme/department leads. Living document.

## Decisions taken (2026-05-30)

1. **Expired deadlines removed.** Several WIGs carried past dates (YeBo "Aug 2025", Top Learners HS "30 Jan 2026", University "April 2026"). Decision: drop the dates and assume **"by end of 2026"** for all targets until the team confirms real deadlines.

2. **Lead measures that are actually outcomes were removed** from lead-measure consideration:
   - ECD Literacy: ~~"75% of children learn 1 letter sound per week"~~
   - Zazi iZandi: ~~"75% of children learn 3 letter sounds per week"~~
   
   Rationale: these are *results* (lag), not behaviours the team directly controls (lead). They belong on the WIG/outcome side. The genuine lead measure is the teaching behaviour that produces them (sessions delivered, teaching the correct letters at the right level). To be re-confirmed with programme leads.

3. **Deferred to a future session** (out of current scope): Top Learners (High School + University), Marketing, Finance, Fundraising. Reason: no backend data exists; these are manual-entry tiles whose data-entry mechanism we'll design later.

4. **Assessment data timing.** 2026 baseline results are being loaded now; midline assessments run over ~the next 2 weeks (~mid-June 2026). v1 is therefore a **lead-measure scoreboard**; WIG outcome (lag) tiles show "awaiting baseline/midline" until data lands.

5. **Bundled WIG display = sub-gauges + roll-up.** A programme with multiple targets (Zazi iZandi 3 grades, Numeracy 2, Core Literacy 2 grades, 1000 Stories 2 framings) renders as one programme tile with one row per target (actual vs its own target + RAG status) and an "X of N on track" roll-up header. Not a single binary met/not-met, and not a blended weighted average. WIG is "met" only when all sub-targets are reached, but progress shows continuously per target.
